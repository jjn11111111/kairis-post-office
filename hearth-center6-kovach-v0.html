How to read the sandbox (aligned function)

Hubs are centroids — each gold ring is the vector-mean longitude of its member bodies (e.g. Hearth = Sun + Venus). That is the chart’s functional summary of that life area, not a movable guess.

Mirrored alignment — hubs ~180° apart face each other across the wheel (Voice ↔ Public in your map). They often describe two sides of one story: inner process vs outer role, private vs visible.

Bound energy — squares and oppositions mark departments that require each other to evolve (Hearth ↔ Bond: home fire and soul-partnership). Tension here is relational glue when honored, not “bad luck.”

Ease — sextiles and trines show natural backup (Belief → Bond: feeling-mind supports the nodal bond path).

White center dot — balance point of all six hubs (~Leo in your natal map): where the whole system leans when every department gets equal weight. Contrast with the full-chart centroid (~Pisces): private myth vs integrated heart-forward stance.

Profection slider — which hub is timed for the birthday year; use focus/compare to see how that year’s theme sits against mirrors and bound pairs.
